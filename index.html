How this is calculated

The engine runs month by month. In Scenario A, PVD receives your employee percentage plus the employer percentage. In Scenario B, your existing investment portfolio receives an independent 0–100% of salary plus optional employer cash. In Scenario C, the same starting portfolio receives no new money. Returns are entered as effective annual rates and converted to equivalent monthly rates.
Comparison guardrail: when the investment addition rate differs from your PVD employee rate, the app flags that the personal savings budgets are different. Use “Match PVD employee rate” for an apples-to-apples employee cash-flow comparison, or keep a different percentage intentionally.
  • Thai PVD contribution limits: both employee and employer contributions are legally set between 2% and 15% of salary.
  • Break-even return: solved numerically — the gross investment return (holding starting balances, additions, tax and fee settings fixed) at which Scenario B equals Scenario A.
  • Employer match forfeited: shown as a future value — what those forgone employer contributions would have grown to by retirement, at the PVD fund return rate.
  • Inflation view: the real-comparison chart divides each year's balances by (1 + inflation)years elapsed. The year-by-year table always shows nominal (undiscounted) values.
